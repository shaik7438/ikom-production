Director

About the Director:

Syed Shah Imran is a young entrepreneur and a graduate of Presidency College, Bangalore. Driven by an uncompromising passion for live experiences, he has built IKOM Productions from the ground up, personally conceptualising, curating and managing every event the company delivers.

In just a few years he has led more than 50 movie promotions and over 150 events, from sold-out shows with Farhan Akhtar, Kapil Sharma and Guru Randhawa to large-scale corporate launches and charity fund raisers. Every one of them has been delivered without a hitch, and every client has come back with glowing feedback.

His vision is simple: to bring every part of event management under one roof, so that every show, launch and celebration is coordinated, controlled and delivered by a single trusted team.
